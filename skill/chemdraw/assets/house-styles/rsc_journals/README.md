# Royal Society of Chemistry house style

How reaction schemes are drawn in RSC journals (Chemical Science, Chemical Communications, Organic & Biomolecular Chemistry, Green Chemistry), measured from open-access figures. Machine-readable card: `toolkit/cdxml_toolkit/publication/house_styles/rsc_journals.json` (style slug `rsc_journals`, base preset `rsc`).

## Sources (CC BY 3.0)

- Cen et al., Chem. Sci. 2024, DOI 10.1039/d4sc05429f: Table 2 scheme (`evidence/rsc_journals-01.png`).
- Freiberg et al., Chem. Sci. 2023, 14, 3462, DOI 10.1039/d3sc00198a: Scheme 1 (`-02`) and Scheme 6 (`-03`).

## Measurements (mean of three crops, 300 dpi page renders)

- Bond length / atom-label cap height: 1.91.
- Line width / bond length: 0.046.
- Arrow shaft / bond length: 7.7 (range 4.5 to 11.9).
- Atom labels: Arial or Helvetica, about 8 pt at print size.

## Conventions

- Compound numbers bold black in both papers (one scheme in the second paper uses plain numbers).
- Reagents and catalysts above the arrow; solvent and temperature below (both papers).
- Kekulé rings (both papers).
- Plus sign between substrates (both papers; inline in one, stacked in the other).
- Reagent text black; plain arrow.
- Abbreviated groups seen in both papers: OMe, CF3, OCF3.
- Not settled by two papers: yield format (77% (84%) versus (94%)), new-bond colour (magenta #B11F59 versus red #F40405), substrate colouring, grey header panel.
