# Science / Science Advances house style

How reaction schemes are drawn in Science and Science Advances, measured from open-access figures. Machine-readable card: `toolkit/cdxml_toolkit/publication/house_styles/science_aaas.json` (base preset `nature`; the journal uses similar small sans-serif labels).

## Sources (CC BY 4.0)

- Choudhary, Zhang and Lin, Sci. Adv. 2024, 10, eadp7544, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adp7544: Fig. 3 (`evidence/science_aaas-01.png`) and Fig. 4 (`-02`).
- Zhao et al., Sci. Adv. 2024, 10, eadq0028, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adq0028: Fig. 3 (`-03`).

## Measurements (mean of three crops, 300 dpi page renders)

- Bond length / atom-label cap height: 1.94.
- Line width / bond length: 0.052.
- Arrow shaft / bond length: 12.8 (range 7.7 to 15.5).
- Atom labels: Arial or Helvetica, about 8 pt at print size.

## Conventions

- Compound numbers bold black, suffix not italic (both papers).
- Yield as a plain percentage after the number on the same line: 3a, 88% or 2a: 89% (both papers).
- The incoming component (amine, bromine) blue, about #0B0AED, in reagents and products; the rest black (both papers).
- Kekulé rings (both papers).
- Reagent text black; plain arrow.
- Abbreviated groups seen in both papers: OMe, CF3.
- Not settled by two papers: where the solvent sits (above in one paper, below in the other), red new bond (#F40405), bold wedge width, inline A + B layout.
