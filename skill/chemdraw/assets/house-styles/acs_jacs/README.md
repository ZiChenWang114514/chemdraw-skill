# ACS organic journals house style (`acs_jacs`)

How reaction schemes are drawn in J. Am. Chem. Soc., Org. Lett., J. Org. Chem. and JACS Au, measured from open-access figures. It adds drawing conventions on top of the ACS Document 1996 style sheet (`acs1996`).

## Sources (all CC BY 4.0)

1. Zhao, Gu and Wu, Org. Lett. 2024, 26, 10702, DOI 10.1021/acs.orglett.4c03519 (crop 01)
2. Qing et al., Org. Lett. 2024, 26, 9215, DOI 10.1021/acs.orglett.4c03212 (crops 02, 03)
3. Iyer et al., JACS Au 2024, 4, 680, DOI 10.1021/jacsau.3c00742 (crop 04; a web figure image of unknown print scale, so its ratios are approximate)

## Conventions

- Compound numbers bold and black, under the structure: 01, 02, 03, 04
- Yield as a bare percentage after the bold compound number, separated by a comma (7e, 73%): 02, 03. Crop 04 puts the yield on a line under the label (X = Br, 91%, 45 min), so placement varies.
- Catalyst or oxidant above the arrow; solvent, temperature and time below: 01, 02, 03, 04. The base sits below the arrow in 01 (DIPEA) and 04 (KOtBu).
- Substrates side by side with a plus sign: 01, 02, 03, 04
- Groups drawn as labels: OTMS, Me, Ph, CO2Me, CN and NO2 in 02; OMe in 04. No group is drawn as a label in two crops, so no abbreviation list is set. The MeO in 02 appears only inside substituent names in the text.
- Aromatic rings as Kekule structures: 01, 02, 03, 04
- Plain thin arrow: 01, 02, 03, 04
- Reagent and condition text black: 01, 02, 03, 04. Two exceptions are annotations, not reagent text: blue-grey italic section headers (02, 03) and the single blue word "ocean" in one condition line (04).
- Sans-serif labels (Arial or Helvetica): 01, 02, 03, 04
- Bond about 2.2 times the capital height of atom labels: 01, 02, 03, 04 (2.1 to 2.3 across crops). Line about 0.055 of the bond: 01, 02, 03, 04.
- Arrow about 11 bonds long in 02 and 04, about 7 in 01 and about 6 in 03. The card's measured value is 11.1, the value of the two crops that agree.

## Colour

- Substrate cores dark red #800000 and the azide and TMS part dark blue #004080: 02 (flat fills) and 03 (same coding). Crop 01 colours the aryl reagent 2a in pure blue #0000FF instead, and crop 04 has no substrate colouring.
- The new bond is purple in 04 only and black in 01 and 03, so no new-bond colour is set.

Colour use differs between papers, so no further colour rule is set.

## Figure types

- Reaction scheme: 01, 02, 03, 04. The top-level conventions apply; the section sets no override.
